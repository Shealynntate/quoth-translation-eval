# Sources of the case sentences

Every sentence and earlier passage in `marks.json` and `haber.json` is quoted from one of the five
Spanish translations below. The quotes come from a cleaned copy of each text, in which spelling
was normalised and transcription errors were repaired, so a quote can differ in small ways from
the source page.

A recorded basis of "worldwide" means the translation was judged to meet two conditions: it
was published in 1930 or earlier, and its translator died in 1945 or earlier. These notes have
not been reviewed by a lawyer.

## Cuentos escogidos

- Original: tales of the Brothers Grimm (Hermanos Grimm).
- Translator: José S. Viedma, a pen form of José Sánchez Biedma.
- Edition: Gaspar, editores, Madrid, 1879.
- Obtained from: Wikisource, https://es.wikisource.org/wiki/Cuentos_escogidos_de_los_Hermanos_Grimm
- Public-domain basis: the translator died about 1898 (Wikidata Q27779392, VIAF 87718972,
  BNE XX868248). That year is weakly sourced, but he was born about 1828 to 1831, so he would have
  had to live to about 115 to survive 1945. Recorded basis: worldwide.

## Cuentos de hadas

- Original: fairy tales by Charles Perrault.
- Translator: Teodoro Baró y Sureda (the title page reads "Traducido por D. TEODORO BARÓ").
- Edition: Bastinos, Barcelona, 1883.
- Obtained from: Wikisource, https://es.wikisource.org/wiki/Cuentos_de_hadas_(Baró_tr.)
  - Two tales, Roquete del Copete and Meñiquín, reached Wikisource as modernised transcriptions
    (one labelled "Trad. J. Coll y Vehí", who died in 1876), whose wording is Baró's.
  - Both were read against the 1883 scan.
- Public-domain basis: the translator died in 1916 (Wikidata Q15629712, VIAF 4087730). Recorded
  basis: worldwide.

## La isla del tesoro

- Original: Treasure Island, by Robert Louis Stevenson.
- Translator: Manuel Caballero.
- Edition: D. Appleton y Compañía, Nueva York, 5th edition, 1901.
- Obtained from: Project Gutenberg, https://www.gutenberg.org/ebooks/45438
- Public-domain basis: the translator died on 3 January 1926 (https://www.elem.mx/autor/datos/2922;
  no Wikidata entity exists). Recorded basis: worldwide.

## La letra escarlata

- Original: The Scarlet Letter, by Nathaniel Hawthorne.
- Translator: Francisco Sellén.
- Edition: D. Appleton y Cía., Nueva York, 3rd edition. Year: not recorded.
- Obtained from: Project Gutenberg, https://www.gutenberg.org/ebooks/36990
- Public-domain basis: the translator died in 1907 (Wikidata Q106594185, VIAF 118455589). Recorded
  basis: worldwide.

## El sabueso de los Baskerville

- Original: The Hound of the Baskervilles, by Arthur Conan Doyle.
- Translator: Arturo Costa Álvarez.
- Edition: not recorded.
- Obtained from: Wikisource, https://es.wikisource.org/wiki/El_sabueso_de_los_Baskerville_(Costa_Álvarez_tr.)
- Public-domain basis: the translator died in 1929 (Wikidata Q5707185, VIAF
  77146824872407631255). In Mexico the work remains protected until 2031, because the term
  there runs from the death of Doyle himself in 1930. Recorded basis: public domain everywhere
  else the two conditions above decide it.
